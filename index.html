Gateway Industrial Arts

Hands-on industrial arts education for youth and adults in St. Louis County

Our Mission

Gateway Industrial Arts is a nonprofit providing youth with creative, hands-on educational experiences that spark imagination, communication, problem-solving, confidence, and exposure to skilled trades and craftsmanship. We also offer adults accessible opportunities, tools, instruction, and community engagement in industrial arts and creative making.

Programs & Activities

We offer workshops, classes, demonstrations, and community programs in:

  • Blacksmithing
  • Metalwork
  • Woodworking
  • Creative fabrication

All youth programs follow strict safety protocols, including required supervision ratios, tool certification, and emergency procedures.
